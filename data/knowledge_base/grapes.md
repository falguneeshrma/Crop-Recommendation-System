# Grapes

## Overview
A perennial woody vine grown for table fruit, raisins, or wine, requiring trellising and a distinct pruning cycle.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 0.0-40.0 (avg 23.2) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 120.0-145.0 (avg 132.5) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 195.0-205.0 (avg 200.1) kg/ha
- Temperature: 8.8-41.9 (avg 23.8) °C
- Humidity: 80.0-84.0 (avg 81.9) %
- Soil pH: 5.5-6.5 (avg 6.0)
- Rainfall: 65.0-74.9 (avg 69.6) mm

## Soil Preference
Well-drained sandy loam; grapevines are sensitive to waterlogged roots.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Balanced NPK with an emphasis on potassium during berry development to improve sugar content and color; timing is tied to the pruning/fruiting cycle.

## Irrigation
Requires controlled deficit irrigation — moderate water during vegetative growth, reduced water near veraison (color change) to concentrate sugars.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Downy mildew, powdery mildew, and thrips are major concerns, especially in humid conditions.

## Harvesting
From pruning to harvest is typically 100-140 days depending on variety and region. Harvest based on sugar content (Brix) rather than color alone.
