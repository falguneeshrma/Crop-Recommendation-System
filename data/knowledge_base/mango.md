# Mango

## Overview
A long-lived tropical/subtropical fruit tree, often called the 'king of fruits', requiring several years to reach full bearing.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 0.0-40.0 (avg 20.1) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 15.0-40.0 (avg 27.2) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 25.0-35.0 (avg 29.9) kg/ha
- Temperature: 27.0-36.0 (avg 31.2) °C
- Humidity: 45.0-55.0 (avg 50.2) %
- Soil pH: 4.5-7.0 (avg 5.8)
- Rainfall: 89.3-100.8 (avg 94.7) mm

## Soil Preference
Deep, well-drained loamy soil; tolerates a range of soil types but not waterlogging.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Moderate, age-graded NPK doses increasing as the tree matures; potassium is important for fruit quality and size, applied post-harvest and pre-flowering.

## Irrigation
Needs a distinct dry period before flowering to induce blossoming; irrigation resumes after fruit set and during fruit development, then tapers before harvest to improve sweetness.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Mango hopper, fruit fly, and powdery mildew (on flowers) are the major threats.

## Harvesting
Fruit matures 3-5 months after flowering. Harvest at physiological maturity (change in fruit shoulder shape and skin color) rather than full ripeness for better transport.
