# Mothbeans

## Overview
An extremely drought-hardy legume traditionally grown in arid and semi-arid regions with minimal inputs.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 0.0-40.0 (avg 21.4) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 35.0-60.0 (avg 48.0) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 15.0-25.0 (avg 20.2) kg/ha
- Temperature: 24.0-32.0 (avg 28.2) °C
- Humidity: 40.0-65.0 (avg 53.2) %
- Soil pH: 3.5-9.9 (avg 6.8)
- Rainfall: 30.9-74.4 (avg 51.2) mm

## Soil Preference
Thrives in sandy and light-textured soils with poor fertility; tolerates drought better than almost any other pulse.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Very low fertilizer requirement; a light phosphorus dose at sowing is usually enough given its nitrogen-fixing ability.

## Irrigation
Almost entirely rainfed; excels in low-rainfall regions where other legumes fail.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Generally pest-hardy; yellow mosaic virus and pod borer can occur in wetter years.

## Harvesting
Short duration crop (60-90 days), making it useful for double-cropping in arid zones.
