# Orange

## Overview
An evergreen citrus tree suited to subtropical climates, sensitive to frost but requiring some cool nights for good fruit color and sugar development.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 0.0-40.0 (avg 19.6) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 5.0-30.0 (avg 16.6) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 5.0-15.0 (avg 10.0) kg/ha
- Temperature: 10.0-34.9 (avg 22.8) °C
- Humidity: 90.0-95.0 (avg 92.2) %
- Soil pH: 6.0-8.0 (avg 7.0)
- Rainfall: 100.2-119.7 (avg 110.5) mm

## Soil Preference
Well-drained sandy loam with slightly acidic pH; very sensitive to waterlogging (root rot risk).

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Balanced NPK in split doses through the growing season, with micronutrients (zinc, iron) often needed on alkaline soils.

## Irrigation
Regular but not excessive irrigation; a brief mild moisture stress before flowering can improve bloom synchronization.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Citrus canker, leaf miner, and fruit fly are common; huanglongbing (citrus greening), spread by psyllids, is a serious modern threat.

## Harvesting
Fruit matures 7-12 months after flowering depending on variety. Harvest based on rind color change and sugar-acid ratio, since fruit doesn't ripen further after picking.
