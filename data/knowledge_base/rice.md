# Rice

## Overview
A staple cereal grown widely in flooded (paddy) or upland systems across South and Southeast Asia. Needs standing water for most of its growth cycle in the paddy method.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 60.0-99.0 (avg 79.9) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 35.0-60.0 (avg 47.6) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 35.0-45.0 (avg 39.9) kg/ha
- Temperature: 20.0-26.9 (avg 23.7) °C
- Humidity: 80.1-85.0 (avg 82.3) %
- Soil pH: 5.0-7.9 (avg 6.4)
- Rainfall: 182.6-298.6 (avg 236.2) mm

## Soil Preference
Prefers clayey or loamy soil with good water retention. Puddled fields are standard for transplanted rice.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Apply nitrogen in split doses (basal + tillering + panicle initiation) to avoid lodging and maximize grain fill. Phosphorus is best applied fully at basal stage; potassium split between basal and panicle stage.

## Irrigation
Requires continuous flooding (2-5 cm standing water) through most stages, with alternate wetting-drying possible to save water without major yield loss.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Common threats: stem borer, brown planthopper, leaf folder, and blast disease (fungal). Rotate varieties and avoid excess nitrogen to reduce blast risk.

## Harvesting
Typically ready 100-150 days after transplanting, depending on variety. Harvest when 80-85% of grains have turned golden yellow.
