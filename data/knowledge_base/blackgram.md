# Blackgram

## Overview
A pulse crop (urad) grown in both kharif and rabi seasons, important for both food and soil-fertility benefits.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 20.0-60.0 (avg 40.0) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 55.0-80.0 (avg 67.5) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 15.0-25.0 (avg 19.2) kg/ha
- Temperature: 25.1-34.9 (avg 30.0) °C
- Humidity: 60.1-70.0 (avg 65.1) %
- Soil pH: 6.5-7.8 (avg 7.1)
- Rainfall: 60.4-74.9 (avg 67.9) mm

## Soil Preference
Performs well on clay loam to loamy soils with good drainage; moderately tolerant of a range of pH.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Light starter nitrogen with a higher phosphorus dose to support nodulation and pod formation.

## Irrigation
Sensitive to both drought and excess moisture; critical irrigation at flowering and pod-filling stages.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Yellow mosaic virus and powdery mildew are common; pod borer also affects yield.

## Harvesting
Matures in 70-90 days; harvest when pods turn black and dry.
