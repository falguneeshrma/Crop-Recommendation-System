# Lentil

## Overview
A cool-season pulse (masoor) grown mainly as a rabi crop, valued for high protein content and soil-enriching nitrogen fixation.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 0.0-40.0 (avg 18.8) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 55.0-80.0 (avg 68.4) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 15.0-25.0 (avg 19.4) kg/ha
- Temperature: 18.1-29.9 (avg 24.5) °C
- Humidity: 60.1-69.9 (avg 64.8) %
- Soil pH: 5.9-7.8 (avg 6.9)
- Rainfall: 35.0-54.9 (avg 45.7) mm

## Soil Preference
Well-drained loamy soil with neutral pH; poor tolerance to waterlogging and salinity.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Low nitrogen requirement; phosphorus is the key input for nodulation and root development.

## Irrigation
Mostly rainfed; if irrigating, avoid excess water which encourages fungal disease and reduces nodulation.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Aphids, pod borer, and rust/wilt diseases are the main concerns; seed treatment reduces soil-borne disease risk.

## Harvesting
Matures in 100-130 days. Harvest when lower pods turn brown and leaves start shedding.
