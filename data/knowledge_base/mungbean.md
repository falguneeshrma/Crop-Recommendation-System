# Mungbean

## Overview
A fast-growing, short-duration legume (green gram) widely used as both a grain crop and a green manure.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 0.0-40.0 (avg 21.0) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 35.0-60.0 (avg 47.3) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 15.0-25.0 (avg 19.9) kg/ha
- Temperature: 27.0-29.9 (avg 28.5) °C
- Humidity: 80.0-90.0 (avg 85.5) %
- Soil pH: 6.2-7.2 (avg 6.7)
- Rainfall: 36.1-59.9 (avg 48.4) mm

## Soil Preference
Well-drained sandy loam to loam soil; sensitive to waterlogging.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Minimal nitrogen needed due to nodulation; phosphorus and a small potassium dose improve pod set.

## Irrigation
Light, frequent irrigation is better than heavy flooding; critical at flowering and pod development.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Yellow mosaic virus (spread by whitefly) is the major threat; also aphids and pod borer.

## Harvesting
Very short duration (60-75 days), allowing it to fit well between two main crops in a rotation.
