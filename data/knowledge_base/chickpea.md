# Chickpea

## Overview
A cool-season pulse (gram) valued for protein content, grown mainly as a rabi (winter) crop in semi-arid regions.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 20.0-60.0 (avg 40.1) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 55.0-80.0 (avg 67.8) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 75.0-85.0 (avg 79.9) kg/ha
- Temperature: 17.0-21.0 (avg 18.9) °C
- Humidity: 14.3-20.0 (avg 16.9) %
- Soil pH: 6.0-8.9 (avg 7.3)
- Rainfall: 65.1-94.8 (avg 80.1) mm

## Soil Preference
Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam with neutral to slightly alkaline pH. Poor tolerance for waterlogging.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Being a legume, it fixes atmospheric nitrogen via root nodules — needs only a small starter dose of nitrogen, with phosphorus being the key nutrient to support nodulation.

## Irrigation
Mostly grown as a rainfed crop; if irrigated, critical stages are branching and pod filling. Avoid irrigation at flowering to reduce flower drop.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Pod borer (Helicoverpa) is the major pest; wilt (Fusarium) is the key disease — use resistant varieties and seed treatment.

## Harvesting
Matures in 90-120 days. Harvest when plants turn yellowish-brown and pods rattle when shaken.
