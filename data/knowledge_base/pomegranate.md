# Pomegranate

## Overview
A hardy, drought-tolerant fruit shrub/small tree suited to semi-arid and Mediterranean-type climates.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 0.0-40.0 (avg 18.9) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 5.0-30.0 (avg 18.8) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 35.0-45.0 (avg 40.2) kg/ha
- Temperature: 18.1-25.0 (avg 21.8) °C
- Humidity: 85.1-95.0 (avg 90.1) %
- Soil pH: 5.6-7.2 (avg 6.4)
- Rainfall: 102.5-112.5 (avg 107.5) mm

## Soil Preference
Adapts to a wide range of soils, including saline and alkaline, but performs best in well-drained loamy soil.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Balanced NPK with higher potassium during fruit development improves fruit size, color, and juice content. Apply farmyard manure annually.

## Irrigation
Drip irrigation is ideal — drought tolerant once established, but consistent moisture during fruit set and development improves fruit quality and reduces cracking.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Fruit borer and bacterial blight are major concerns; fruit cracking is a physiological issue linked to irrigation inconsistency.

## Harvesting
Fruit matures 5-7 months after flowering; harvest when the rind turns from green to pink/red and produces a metallic sound when tapped.
