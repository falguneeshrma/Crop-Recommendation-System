# Jute

## Overview
A fast-growing fiber crop grown mainly in warm, humid, high-rainfall river-delta regions, used to produce natural fiber (burlap/hessian).

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 60.0-100.0 (avg 78.4) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 35.0-60.0 (avg 46.9) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 35.0-45.0 (avg 40.0) kg/ha
- Temperature: 23.1-27.0 (avg 25.0) °C
- Humidity: 70.9-89.9 (avg 79.6) %
- Soil pH: 6.0-7.5 (avg 6.7)
- Rainfall: 150.2-199.8 (avg 174.8) mm

## Soil Preference
Fertile, well-drained alluvial loamy soil with good water-holding capacity.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Moderate nitrogen for vegetative growth (fiber comes from stem tissue), with phosphorus and potassium supporting overall plant vigor.

## Irrigation
High rainfall requirement; often grown as a rainfed monsoon crop, with supplemental irrigation needed if rains are delayed.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Stem weevil, semi-looper caterpillar, and stem rot are common issues in waterlogged conditions.

## Harvesting
Harvested 100-120 days after sowing at the flowering stage, when fiber quality is optimal; stems are then retted in water to separate fiber.
