# Muskmelon

## Overview
A warm-season vining fruit (cantaloupe) similar in cultivation needs to watermelon but generally shorter duration.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 80.0-120.0 (avg 100.3) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 5.0-30.0 (avg 17.7) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 45.0-55.0 (avg 50.1) kg/ha
- Temperature: 27.0-29.9 (avg 28.7) °C
- Humidity: 90.0-95.0 (avg 92.3) %
- Soil pH: 6.0-6.8 (avg 6.4)
- Rainfall: 20.2-29.9 (avg 24.7) mm

## Soil Preference
Sandy loam with good drainage and warmth; poor tolerance for waterlogged conditions.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Balanced NPK during vegetative growth, with increased potassium during fruit maturation for sweetness.

## Irrigation
Regular irrigation through vine growth and fruit set; reduce watering as fruit nears maturity to concentrate sugars and avoid fruit splitting.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Fruit fly and powdery mildew are the primary concerns; downy mildew in humid conditions.

## Harvesting
Matures in 70-90 days. Harvest at 'full slip' stage — the fruit separates easily from the vine with a light twist.
