# Coffee

## Overview
A perennial shrub/small tree grown in tropical highlands, typically under partial shade, taking 3-4 years to reach first bearing.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 80.0-120.0 (avg 101.2) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 15.0-40.0 (avg 28.7) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 25.0-35.0 (avg 29.9) kg/ha
- Temperature: 23.1-27.9 (avg 25.5) °C
- Humidity: 50.0-69.9 (avg 58.9) %
- Soil pH: 6.0-7.5 (avg 6.8)
- Rainfall: 115.2-199.5 (avg 158.1) mm

## Soil Preference
Deep, well-drained, slightly acidic loamy soil rich in organic matter.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Balanced NPK applied in split doses aligned with the flowering-fruiting cycle; potassium is particularly important for bean filling and quality.

## Irrigation
Needs a brief dry spell before flowering to trigger uniform blossoming ('blossom showers'), then consistent moisture through berry development.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Coffee berry borer and leaf rust (a fungal disease) are the most damaging; shade management helps reduce leaf rust pressure.

## Harvesting
Cherries ripen 7-9 months after flowering; harvested selectively as cherries turn red (for arabica) since ripening is uneven on the same branch.
