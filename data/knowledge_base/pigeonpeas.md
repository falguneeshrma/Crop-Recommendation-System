# Pigeonpeas

## Overview
A drought-tolerant perennial legume (tur/arhar) widely grown in semi-arid tropics, often intercropped with cereals.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 0.0-40.0 (avg 20.7) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 55.0-80.0 (avg 67.7) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 15.0-25.0 (avg 20.3) kg/ha
- Temperature: 18.3-37.0 (avg 27.7) °C
- Humidity: 30.4-69.7 (avg 48.1) %
- Soil pH: 4.5-7.4 (avg 5.8)
- Rainfall: 90.1-198.8 (avg 149.5) mm

## Soil Preference
Adapts to a wide range of soils but performs best in well-drained loamy soil; intolerant of waterlogging.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Minimal nitrogen needed due to nitrogen fixation; phosphorus at sowing boosts nodulation and early growth.

## Irrigation
Highly drought tolerant with a deep root system; mostly rainfed, with irrigation only critical at flowering/pod-filling in dry spells.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Pod borer complex (Helicoverpa, Maruca) and wilt disease are major concerns; sterility mosaic virus is also significant.

## Harvesting
Long duration crop (150-270 days depending on variety). Harvest when pods dry and turn brown.
