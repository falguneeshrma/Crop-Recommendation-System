# Kidneybeans

## Overview
A legume grown for its large colorful seeds (rajma), popular in cooler climates and higher elevations.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 0.0-40.0 (avg 20.8) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 55.0-80.0 (avg 67.5) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 15.0-25.0 (avg 20.0) kg/ha
- Temperature: 15.3-24.9 (avg 20.1) °C
- Humidity: 18.1-25.0 (avg 21.6) %
- Soil pH: 5.5-6.0 (avg 5.7)
- Rainfall: 60.3-149.7 (avg 105.9) mm

## Soil Preference
Well-drained loamy soil with good organic matter; sensitive to both drought and waterlogging.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Light nitrogen starter dose since it's a nitrogen-fixing legume; phosphorus and potassium support pod development and seed size.

## Irrigation
Needs consistent moisture, especially at flowering and pod-filling; avoid water stress during these windows.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Bean beetle, aphids, and anthracnose/bacterial blight are common issues. Use certified disease-free seed.

## Harvesting
Ready in 90-120 days. Harvest dry beans when pods rattle and have fully dried on the plant.
