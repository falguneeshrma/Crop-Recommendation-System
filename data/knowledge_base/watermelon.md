# Watermelon

## Overview
A warm-season vining fruit crop that spreads extensively and needs plenty of space and heat.

## Ideal Growing Conditions (from historical field data, 100 samples)
- Nitrogen (N) in soil: 80.0-120.0 (avg 99.4) kg/ha
- Phosphorus (P) in soil: 5.0-30.0 (avg 17.0) kg/ha
- Potassium (K) in soil: 45.0-55.0 (avg 50.2) kg/ha
- Temperature: 24.0-27.0 (avg 25.6) °C
- Humidity: 80.0-90.0 (avg 85.2) %
- Soil pH: 6.0-7.0 (avg 6.5)
- Rainfall: 40.1-59.8 (avg 50.8) mm

## Soil Preference
Well-drained sandy loam warms quickly and suits its heat-loving nature; sensitive to waterlogging.

## Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
Moderate nitrogen early for vine growth, shifting to higher potassium during fruit development for sweetness; avoid excess nitrogen late, which delays fruiting.

## Irrigation
Needs consistent moisture through vine growth and fruit set, but reduced watering near harvest improves sugar concentration.

## Common Pests & Diseases
Fruit fly, aphids (which spread viruses), and powdery mildew are common issues.

## Harvesting
Matures 80-100 days after sowing. Harvest indicators: dried tendril nearest the fruit, dull skin sheen, and a hollow sound when tapped.
